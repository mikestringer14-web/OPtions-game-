Promotion Terms

Plain language. Read these before you send us a card.

What this is

This is a business to business loss leader marketing promotion. We buy physical trading cards above market value to build publicity for our brand and our documentary. You are selling us a card. That's the whole transaction.

What this is not
  • Not an investment. You don't get shares, equity, profit sharing or any promised return.
  • Not a contest, lottery or game of chance. No purchase or entry fee is ever required to sell to us.
  • Not a loan or a grant. There's nothing to pay back and no reporting back to us.
What you do with the money

The promotion is built to help small businesses and people starting one. We'd love to hear what you build with it. But once the sale is paid, the money is yours and how you use it is completely up to you. We don't control it and we're not responsible for it.

Who can sell
  • 18 or older, with government photo ID matching the payee.
  • You must legally own the card. We check authenticity and may require a grading company certificate.
  • Payment is made only to the verified seller or their registered business, by traceable method (e-transfer, bank transfer or cheque). No cash.
  • We may decline any card or any seller for any reason, including authenticity, ownership or compliance concerns.
Record claims

Our "on record" claims are based on the highest publicly documented sale of a single English language energy card we could verify at the time of each buy. Sources are published with each buy. If a higher verified sale exists, we update the ladder.

Filming and limits

Record buys may be filmed for the documentary. Appearing on camera is optional and needs a separate signed release. Ladder prices, tiers and unlocks can change or end at any time before a sale is agreed in writing. Agreed sales are honoured.
