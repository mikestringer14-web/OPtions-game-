Welcome to OPtions! 🚀
How to Play:
• Watch the stock price chart move in real-time
• Buy Call options if you think price will go UP
• Buy Put options if you think price will go DOWN
• Close positions anytime to lock in profit/loss
Understanding Greeks:
• Delta: How much option price changes with stock
• Theta: How much you lose per day (time decay)
Game Modes:
• Practice: Learn at your own pace
• Tournament: 5-minute speed challenge
• Campaign: Progressive difficulty levels
⚠️ This is an educational game. Real options trading involves significant risk.
